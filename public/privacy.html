Privacy Policy

This application follows data minimization principles.

We store only the data needed for functionality:

  • username
  • user ID
  • game results
  • rulesets

Your data is used only for gameplay and identification within the app. No data is shared with third parties.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting your account. This will remove all associated personal data.

No sensitive personal data (such as passwords) is stored.
